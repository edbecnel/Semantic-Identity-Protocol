Core UX rule The distinction the dropdown must make clear.

Typing categories explores meaning.
Choosing an owner-bound result commits identity.

The owner, such as stackworks, is not treated as a child category under Lasca. It appears as a separate owner-bound candidate and, when selected, rewrites the whole SIP string. Shortcut SIPs such as @games.lasca begin as discovery queries; the resolver can show likely registered identities, and the user can save one as a personal shortcut. Owner-bound shortcuts such as @stackworks.games.lasca can resolve or expand to the canonical owner-qualified SIP. Synonyms such as columns and stacking can be treated as equivalent semantic path segments within a narrow context. Broader paths such as @games.strategy.abstract.stacking are handled as broad semantic facets that may match multiple narrower branches rather than collapsing into one canonical path.
